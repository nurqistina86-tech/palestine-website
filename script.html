Introduction to Palestine

Palestine is a historic land with a rich history, culture and heritage.It is home to many important historical and religious places

Palestine is known for its beautiful cities, traditional food,ancient architecture and strong cultural traditions

Explore Governorates

Palestinian Governorates

Palestine has several governorates with unique history, culture and important places

Jerusalem

Jerusalem is a historic city known for its important religious and cultural sites

Gaza

Gaza is a coastal region with a long history and rich Palestinian culture

Hebron

Hebron is an ancient city famous for its historical places and traditional markets.

Nablus

Nablus is known for its historic old city, traditional food and cultural heritage.

Bethlehem

Bethlehem is a historic city with important religious and cultural landmarks.

Jericho

Jericho is one of the world's oldest cities and has a very long history.

Palestinian Culture

Traditional Food

Palestinian cuisine includes traditional dishes such as maqluba,musakhan,falafel and hummus.

Traditional Clothing

Palestinian traditional clothing includes beautiful embroidered dresses called thobes.

Traditional Music

Music and traditional dances such as Dabke are important parts of Palestinian culture.
